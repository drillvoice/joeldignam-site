Joel Dignam

Founder of Better Renting. Veteran strategist, campaigner, and organiser on housing, energy, and climate.

Joel Dignam founded Better Renting in Canberra in 2018 and led it as Executive Director until 2025, building a community of renters working for stable, affordable and healthy homes. Better Renting campaigned successfully for the ACT to end no-cause evictions and set minimum insulation standards for rentals, and its research received national media coverage year on year, helping to shape the agenda on rental issues. A 2019 Churchill Fellow, Joel now works at the Summer Foundation, leading the Building Better Homes campaign.

LinkedIn profile

Highlights

  • Founded Better Renting in 2018 and led it as Executive Director until 2025, growing it into a national voice for people who rent.
  • Won policy change in the ACT: an end to no-cause evictions and minimum ceiling insulation standards for rental homes.
  • Created Renter Researchers, a citizen-science project tracking temperatures in 70+ rental homes; its findings drove national coverage, including on the ABC.
  • Led reports including Rent due (2020), which shaped the conversation on support for renters during COVID-19 and was cited by the Reserve Bank.
  • Awards: 2019 Churchill Fellowship (tenant movements in the US and UK), 2020 Gill Owen Scholarship (Energy Consumers Australia), and first place in the 2018 Gill Owen Essay Prize.
  • More than 240 media appearances and 18 op-eds since 2010, across the ABC, The Guardian, The Australian, SBS, The Sydney Morning Herald, the AFR, The Canberra Times and others.
